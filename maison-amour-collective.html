The manifesto

Welcome to the anti-cynical movement.

We live in a world that conditioned us to believe that to be taken seriously, we had to become boring. We’ve been taught to dim our lights, wear gray coats, and treat love like a transactional marketplace where we hold back our vulnerability out of fear.

Maison Amour is the cage break.

Born as a home on the tiny Thai island of Koh Phangan, and now dropping anchor in the vibrant heart of Amsterdam, this is a nomadic collective for the conscious creatives, the seekers, and the high-performers who refuse to live half a life.

We believe that you can be highly efficient, wildly successful, deeply philosophical, and still be awkwardly joyful, madly in love with life, and unapologetically fun.

Presence is the new luxury.

The vibe

Wild & Wise

We don’t do boring networking. We don’t do beige spirituality. This is punk-conscious. We create beautiful, embodied experiences where the heart meets the party.

Wild

Celebration

High-vibe social mixers, rising artists showcasing their raw work, DJ sets, and a touch of rose-colored glasses. We bypass the small talk. No one cares what you do for a living here; we care about who you are when you take off your social armor.

Wise

Philosophy & connection

Zero-compromise philosophy talks on conditioning and expectations, backed by neuroscience. Self-inquiry and conscious connection that take you out of your comfort zone. Expect lighter shoulders, wet eyes, and a pocket full of truths.
